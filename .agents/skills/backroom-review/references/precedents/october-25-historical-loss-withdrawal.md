## Historical law and live eligibility are separate

This is a dated operator withdrawal and current reconsideration instruction;
it does not claim that the old policy text always said the same thing, rewrite
v10–v12 prompt text, erase original rulings, or activate another policy/private
audit. Retain the prior policy/digest/reason and record a new audited action
under the operator's current authority. Current native court status remains
the eligibility readback until the authorized correction lands.

ATH corrections require fresh UUID/SHA/status/score/review guards and audited
reopen/clear readback for each applicable row. This entry does not claim all
17 corrections have landed or clear unrelated paths.
