# Common lesson corpus

`common_lessons.txt` is a reviewed, modern-language subset of the CC0
`data/words/proverbs.json` corpus from `dariusk/corpora`, pinned at upstream
commit `f21f258130c3e02e11b8b99f27c015d7f430802d`.

Source: https://github.com/dariusk/corpora/blob/f21f258130c3e02e11b8b99f27c015d7f430802d/data/words/proverbs.json

License: CC0 1.0. The subset excludes archaic, discriminatory, violent, and
otherwise unsuitable entries and normalizes punctuation for conversational use.
The benchmark never fetches this corpus at runtime.
