# Bench v16 qualification and rollout

Status: **OFF, shadow only.** The v16 contract ships (every layer supports and
advertises 16), but nothing at v16 sets weights. Bench v15 stays the active
scoring contract. Activation is a separate, guarded operator rollout
(`start_benchmark_rollout` desired 16) that needs the phases below and an
operator decision.

## What v16 is

V16 is the dueling bench
([`dueling-bench.md`](../research/dittobench-datagen/docs/dueling-bench.md)).
A canonical run is two halves scored as one composite:

1. **Datagen half (175 cases).** The v15 contract at the dueling profile (50
   tool + 125 memory cases at `full`), with connector tools that no longer say
   whether they are linked, a seeded set of linked connectors that some cases
   need, and wording decoys (section 8.1). Graded and gated exactly as v15.
2. **Generator slice (175 cases).** Five columns of 35 cases from the season's
   pinned generators (the top eligible miner generators, one per owner, with
   the public default generator in any empty slot). Platform's trusted
   `challenge-scorer` builds the slice for each `(season, run_seed)` and
   validators forward it to `/v2/score` as `dueling-slice-v1`.

`composite = gate_datagen x ((1 - 0.1 x |U|) x datagen + 0.1 x sum of
unmasked columns)`. A column owned by the scored agent's coldkey is masked,
and its 10% returns to the datagen half. The dethrone test is pair-masked.
Gates, efficiency and outlier inputs see the datagen half only.

Pack policy 16 ships with it (tandem upgrade). A v16 round and a v16 season
accept packs declaring 15 or 16. Policy 16 grades like 15.

## Prerequisites before any v16 slice exists

1. **Release is live, layer by layer.** Every validator's scorer advertises 16
   and `dueling-slice-v1` (Backroom `get_validator_fleet`:
   `dueling_slice_bench_versions` contains 16); `get_benchmark_rollout_control`
   shows a v16 `capable_validator_count`; `get_generator_scorer` shows the
   deployed generator scorer advertises `dueling_work_kinds=slice,battery` and
   policy 16.
2. **Re-register the public default generator** from generator kit version 5
   (`create_default_generator_upload`, then `register_default_generator`).
   The currently registered kit (version 4) declares at most policy 14, so it
   fails every v16 season battery and cannot fill or substitute a v16 column.
   Even a default-only shadow season needs this.
3. **Shadow seasons without a rollout.** A season opens only while
   `dueling_enabled` is on and a v16 contract is active, is an open rollout's
   desired version, or is the generator competition setting
   `dueling_shadow_bench_version` (#3350). Platform accepts that setting only
   for a shipped contract, so it can be set to 16 once this release is live.
   Shadow seasons are never authoritative; clearing the setting closes the
   open shadow season on the next tick.

| Phase | What happens | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Contract, OFF | This release. Every layer supports and advertises 16; no season opens and no ticket leases at 16. | Release advertises 16; v2 to v15 vectors unchanged. |
| 2. Shadow canary | Default-only shadow season, then `issue_benchmark_canary` at 16 (procedure below), then a season with miner columns. | 3. Shadow rollout | `start_benchmark_rollout` desired 16 (operator approval only). ## Canary procedure (phase 2)

1. Confirm the prerequisites above (release live, default generator
   re-registered).
2. `set_generator_competition_settings` with `dueling_enabled: true` and
   `dueling_shadow_bench_version: 16` (mode not `off`; `rounds_per_season x
   epochs_per_round >= 2`). The source release policy must not be
   `disclosure = never`.
3. Wait for `get_dueling_season` to show an **open shadow season** at bench 16.
   A season commits before its anchor round and opens at the next round
   boundary, so allow up to two rounds (about 9.6 h). Check its battery
   results; the first season may be default-only.
4. `issue_benchmark_canary` at 16 on one validator that forwards 16 in
   `dueling_slice_bench_versions`, against a released top agent.
5. Confirm, as separate facts:
   - `list_dueling_slices` shows the canary's preparation `ready` with its
     latency and digest; a lease before then answered 503 with `Retry-After`;
   - the canary completed with `details.dueling` naming the season and
     `slice_sha256`, five columns, and a composite equal to the fold;
   - per-column means are plausible, and `get_dueling_season` aggregates the
     score.
6. Repeat on two more validators, then on a season with miner columns.

## Rollout start blockers (enforced)

`get_benchmark_rollout_control` lists these for v16, in order:

- the inference proxy configuration;
- the decision lane enabled with a pinned model (v15 and later);
- `dueling_enabled` with the generator competition on, and a source release
  policy other than `disclosure = never` (v16 and later, on every call path);
- capacity: enough fresh, identity-matched v16 validators. That means a scorer
  that advertises 16, carries the version-bound `v16_scored_runtime_env`
  packet, and forwards 16 in `dueling_slice_bench_versions`.

## Open activation blockers

Each must be closed, or waived by the operator, before activation.

- **KOTH pair-mask parity on Platform.** Done in #3351: Platform `koth.py`
  pair-masks and season-isolates the v16 projection like the validator fold
  (#3343). Recheck the shared crown fixture before activation.
- **v16 KOTH constants.** `KOTH_MARGIN`, `KOTH_BAND_DECAY_START_COMPOSITE` and
  the statistical band cap need v16 values from shadow calibration, gated on
  `bench_version >= 16`. Recheck `TOP5_MIN_CONFIRMATION_SEEDS` too: 35-case
  columns are noisier per seed.
- **Generator source release.** A miner column's cases are revealed only after
  that generator's source is released (embargo, `privacy_window_rounds`).
  Source release must be live before any miner generator column enters a
  season that can be revealed.
- **Outlier and anomaly thresholds** recalibrated on the v16 shadow
  distribution.
- **Announcement.** At least 24 hours' notice in #118 for agent miners, and at
  least 7 days for generator miners (tandem upgrade runbook).
- **Generator track share and upload fee**, through governance, together with
  activation.

## Rollback

v15 stays the active contract and is still served throughout. Before
activation, rollback is cancelling the rollout (`cancel_benchmark_canary` for a
canary) and turning `dueling_enabled` off. An open season closes once v16 is
neither active nor rolling out. `rotate_dueling_season` with `immediate: true`
closes a bad season at once. After activation, rollback is a rollout back to
v15. Generator rounds move to 16 only after activation and a validator
quorum; a round at 16 whose validators were drained is voided
(`void_generator_round`).

## After activation

Move the miner rehearsal's `LIVE_SCORING_BENCH_VERSION` and the `mine` skill's
live bench to 16 in the post-activation PR
(`test_miner_docs_quote_the_rehearsal_live_fallback`), and record the
activation here.
